Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. Are my photos or videos uploaded to any remote servers?

No, never. AI Cleaner and Media Recover is built with an absolute 100% on-device architecture. All image scanning, duplicate calculation, blurry detection, and AI enhancements execute strictly on your iPhone's local processor. We do not operate external media servers, and we never view or transmit your personal memories.

2. How do I cancel my subscription or manage renewal?

All in-app purchases and subscriptions are handled securely by Apple StoreKit. To manage or cancel your subscription at any time:
1. Open the Settings app on your iPhone.
2. Tap your Apple ID / Name at the top.
3. Tap Subscriptions.
4. Select AI Cleaner and Media Recover.
5. Tap Cancel Subscription.
If cancelled, you will retain PRO access until the end of the current billing cycle.

3. How can I request a refund?

Because all billing is handled exclusively by Apple Media Services, developers do not possess the ability to issue refunds directly. To request a refund, please visit Apple's official refund portal at reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple ID, find your purchase, and select "Request a refund".

4. What happens when I delete photos using "Swipe to Clean"?

Deleted photos are safely relocated to your native iOS "Recently Deleted" album. Apple protects these items for 30 days before permanent purging. If you delete a photo by mistake, you can simply open the Photos app, tap Albums > Recently Deleted, and restore it back to your camera roll.

5. How does Media Recovery work?

Our on-device recovery engine scans accessible PhotoKit indexes, orphaned file system cache blocks, and recently removed media containers to help surface items that can still be reconstructed. However, recovery depends on whether deleted flash memory blocks have been overwritten by iOS or other apps. We always recommend maintaining regular iCloud backups.

6. How does the Private Media Vault protect my files?

Files in the Vault are encrypted using AES-256 and placed inside the application's isolated sandbox. Access requires your 4-digit PIN or Face ID authentication through Apple's Secure Enclave. We do not store backdoors or master keys; your files are entirely private to you.
