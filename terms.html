2. Scope of Service & Utility Capabilities

The Application is an on-device utility designed to assist users in:

  • Locating duplicate, similar, blurry, or oversized photos and videos across their local iOS Photo Library.
  • Executing user-directed batch deletion and organization of media files into the iOS "Recently Deleted" album.
  • Scanning accessible system storage sectors, local application caches, and orphaned fragments to assist in recovering lost or deleted media records.
  • Enhancing image resolution and tone locally using Core Image and Vision machine learning pipelines.
  • Securing selected personal photos and videos in an on-device encrypted container ("Private Media Vault") protected by AES-256 and Face ID.
