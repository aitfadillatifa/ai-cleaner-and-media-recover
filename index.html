About the Application

AI Cleaner and Media Recover is an all-in-one iOS utility engineered to help users reclaim storage and recover valuable media safely:

  • Primary Media Recovery: Deep on-device scanning of photo libraries and local storage cache fragments.
  • Swipe to Clean: Fluid card-based deck to quickly keep or trash photos and videos.
  • Duplicate & Similar Photo Cleaner: Identifies redundant, identical, and burst sequence captures.
  • AI Photo Enhancer: Sharpens blurry photos, cleans noise, and upscales resolution locally.
  • Private Media Vault: Financial-grade AES-256 encryption protected by Face ID and PIN.
  • Real-Time Hardware Telemetry: Live battery health, temperature state, and storage analysis.

Built for iOS 18+: Crafted with Swift, SwiftUI, PhotoKit, and Apple Vision. No external cloud dependencies or server-side media processing.
